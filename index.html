My story

I started in 2004 on a help desk in Manila, fixing PCs and printers. Then I spent ten years supporting business networks for a major US carrier, working MPLS, VPN and edge router cases with help desks and NOCs across the US, Europe and Asia, and becoming the person escalations landed on.

In 2022 I moved to Dubai and helped build IT from the ground up for a 3,000+ person office across four floors, as one of a three-person team. Since 2023 I have been the only IT person for a company with seven offices, reporting to senior leadership. I moved its headquarters with zero downtime and support its people wherever they work.

Along the way I started building my own tools to work faster and more consistently. I work by one simple rule: process over outcomes. Get the process right and the results follow.
